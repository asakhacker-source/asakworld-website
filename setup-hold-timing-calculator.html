Interpretation and common mistakes

Slack of zero or above is PASS in this simplified model; negative slack is a VIOLATION. A later capture clock helps the stated setup equation but hurts the stated hold equation. Sign conventions differ by report and tool, so inspect the constraints before comparing numbers.

Do not use this page as a signoff result. Confirm min/max libraries, corners, clock definitions, exceptions, asynchronous crossings and generated-clock behavior in an STA flow.
