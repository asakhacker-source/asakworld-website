Space Technology

Space technology helps us observe Earth, communicate across distances and investigate the wider universe. Its benefits come with a duty to use orbit responsibly.

In this guide

Satellite technologyLaunch vehiclesExploration & astronomyEarth observationSustainable space

Space systems support everyday services on Earth while creating new ways to study distant worlds. They are built from specialised hardware, communication networks, sensors and scientific collaboration.

1. Satellite Technology & Communication

Satellites support navigation, weather forecasting, communication, environmental monitoring, disaster management and scientific research. They collect observations and relay information where terrestrial networks are limited.

2. Launch Vehicles

Rockets carry satellites, spacecraft and scientific instruments beyond Earth. Launch systems require careful engineering, testing and mission planning to operate safely and reliably.

3. Planetary Exploration & Astronomy

Robotic spacecraft, rovers and telescopes help researchers study planets, moons, stars and galaxies. They can investigate conditions that are too distant or dangerous for people to reach directly.

4. Earth Observation

Observing Earth from orbit helps scientists track weather, crops, forests, oceans and changing environments. These insights support research and informed responses to natural hazards.

5. Sustainable Space Technology

Responsible space development includes managing orbital debris, coordinating traffic, designing efficient spacecraft and planning missions with long-term effects in mind. Space is a shared environment.
