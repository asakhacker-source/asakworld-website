Privacy, with clarity.

This policy explains how ASARK handles information when you use the website, its analytics controls, advertising, affiliate links and account features.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

What ASARK is

ASARK is an independent editorial website exploring technology, design and the future through guides, visual studies and curated resources.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ASARK and its service providers may receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, referring pages and interaction data. This information helps us understand how the website is used and improve it.

Google Analytics and your consent

ASARK uses Google Analytics 4 to measure website use. Analytics is designed to load only after you make a choice through the website’s analytics consent controls. You can change your choice through the Privacy Choices control where it is available. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google’s own policies.

Cookies and browser storage

ASARK may use cookies, local storage or similar browser storage for preferences, analytics-consent choices and the operation of account features. Browser storage can be managed through your browser settings. Removing it may reset preferences or require you to sign in again.

Advertising

ASARK may display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and other third-party advertising providers may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to collect information as a result of ad serving on ASARK. These technologies may be used to deliver, measure and improve advertising in accordance with the provider's policies, applicable law and your consent choices.

Amazon Associates and affiliate links

Some ASARK pages include links to Amazon.in. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Purchases made through these links may support ASARK at no additional cost to you. When you follow an affiliate link, Amazon and other linked services may collect information under their own policies.

Third-party links

ASARK links to websites and services operated by others. We do not control their content, security practices or privacy policies. Review the policy of any third-party website before providing information there.

Accounts and Supabase authentication

ASARK account features use Supabase authentication. When you create or use an account, authentication-related information such as your email address and account session data is processed by Supabase to provide sign-up, sign-in, password recovery and session management. Do not share your password with anyone.

Data security

We use reasonable technical measures provided by the website and its service providers, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Use the website and any linked services with appropriate care.

Your privacy choices

You can manage analytics consent through ASARK’s Privacy Choices control where available, manage cookies and local storage in your browser, and choose whether to use account features or third-party links.

Children’s privacy

ASARK is not directed to children. Do not submit personal information through the website if you are not permitted to do so under the laws that apply to you.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when ASARK’s website features or relevant practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact ASARK through the Contact page.
